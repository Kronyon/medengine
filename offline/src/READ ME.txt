medengine.exe  v0.8.1 beta
==========================

HOW TO OPEN
1. Unzip this folder and keep the files together (don't move osce.html,
   study.html or the img folder out of it).
2. Double-click medengine.html. It opens in your web browser.
3. Press 1 (or click) for medengine-OSCE, 2 for medengine-STUDY.

No sign-in, no claude.ai. Everything runs from this folder, offline.

THE AI PATIENT, VOICE STATIONS AND EXAMINER MARKING
These need an internet connection and your own Anthropic API key:
1. Go to console.anthropic.com, add some credit, then API keys > Create key.
2. In medengine-OSCE, press "AI key" (top right), paste the key, Save, Test.
The key stays in your browser on this computer and is only ever sent to
api.anthropic.com. You pay Anthropic directly for what you use.
Everything else (scripts, frameworks, hooks, friend practice, self-marking,
all of STUDY) works with no key and no internet.

VOICE
Use Chrome or Edge for the voice stations; allow the microphone when asked.

YOUR PROGRESS
Saved in your browser for this folder. Use the same browser each time.
Progress from the claude.ai versions does not carry over.

For education and exam practice only; not medical advice. Always follow
current UK guidance (NICE, BNF) and your medical school's teaching.
