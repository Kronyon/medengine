@@ di | 📊 | Data interpretation: ECG, X-ray, gas, bloods | Same sentence order for every result: who and what, is it adequate, the systematic read, the most striking abnormality, pertinent negatives, summary, next step.

step: main | !The most striking abnormality first | The most striking abnormality is ___, which is ___. | Leading with the key finding shows clinical reasoning and saves time.
step: neg | Pertinent negatives | There is no ___. | Pertinent negatives show you looked for the dangerous alternatives (no free air, no pneumothorax).
step: sum | !Summary sentence and diagnosis | In summary, this ___ shows ___, consistent with ___. | The reasoning sentence again: finding, meaning, diagnosis.
step: next | Act on it: escalate, treat, repeat | Given this, I'd ___ and discuss with my senior now. | Interpretation without action scores less. Say what you'd do.

nn: The most striking abnormality first

@ cxr | Chest X-ray | PP15, PP18, PP100, PP97 | 9, 11, 19, 21
use: Interpret a CXR.
ch: tech | RIPE: rotation (clavicles equidistant from spinous processes), inspiration (5–6 anterior ribs), projection (PA or AP), exposure (vertebrae just visible behind the heart).
ch: sys | ABCDE: airway (trachea, carina), breathing (lung zones, pleura), cardiac (size under 50% on PA, borders), diaphragm (free air, angles), everything else (bones, tubes, soft tissue); review areas (apices, behind the heart, below the diaphragm).

tree: White out | effusion (meniscus, trachea away); collapse (trachea towards); consolidation (air bronchograms); pneumonectomy
tree: Black | pneumothorax (no lung markings, visible edge); emphysema (hyperinflation)
tree: Heart failure | ABCDE: alveolar oedema, Kerley B lines, cardiomegaly, upper lobe diversion, effusions
nn: Check the review areas
pearl: RIPE, then ABCDE.
