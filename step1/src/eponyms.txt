# medengine-STEP1 eponym decoder: names that do not describe what they are. Written fresh.
# @ep Name | aliases ; ... · f: what you see · h: a hook that ties the name to the picture · x: the exam trap · m: what to do · u: topics

@ep Kimmelstiel-Wilson nodules | KW nodules ; nodular glomerulosclerosis
f: Round pink nodules of mesangial matrix inside the glomeruli, PAS positive
h: **K**immelstiel-**W**ilson: the **K**idney **W**ith sugar
x: Amyloid can also form nodules, but it stains with Congo red and gives apple-green birefringence
m: Diabetic nephropathy: control glucose and BP, ACE inhibitor or ARB, SGLT2 inhibitor
u: Nephrotic syndrome ; Diabetes mellitus

@ep Tamm-Horsfall protein | uromodulin
f: The glycoprotein made by the thick ascending limb that forms the matrix of every urinary cast
h: **T**amm-**H**orsfall comes from the **T**hick ascending limb and holds casts together
x: Pure Tamm-Horsfall casts are hyaline casts, which can be normal in concentrated urine
m: Read what is trapped in the matrix: red cells, white cells, dead tubular cells, fat or wax
u: Acute kidney injury and urinary casts ; Loop of Henle and Bartter syndrome

@ep Fanconi syndrome | proximal tubulopathy
f: Glucose, amino acids, phosphate, urate and bicarbonate all leaking out of the proximal tubule
h: **Fanconi**: the proximal **con**veyor belt has stopped
x: Not Fanconi anaemia, which is a DNA repair defect with marrow failure
m: Look for myeloma, Wilson disease, lead, ifosfamide, cystinosis or expired tetracyclines
u: Proximal convoluted tubule ; Renal tubular acidosis

@ep Potter sequence | oligohydramnios sequence
f: Flat face, beaked nose, low ears, limb contractures and small lungs after too little amniotic fluid
h: A **Potter** shapes clay by squeezing it: the baby is moulded by the uterus with no fluid to cushion it
x: The lungs, not the kidneys, are what kill (pulmonary hypoplasia)
m: Find why no urine was made: bilateral renal agenesis, ARPKD, posterior urethral valves
u: Renal embryology and anatomy ; Cystic kidney disease

@ep Goodpasture syndrome | anti-GBM disease with lung haemorrhage
f: Coughing blood and haematuria together, with linear IgG along the basement membrane
h: A **good pasture** feeds two organs: lungs and kidneys share type IV collagen
x: ANCA vasculitis also hits lung and kidney, but its biopsy is pauci-immune
m: Plasmapheresis, steroids and cyclophosphamide
u: Nephritic syndrome ; Hypersensitivity types

@ep Alport syndrome | hereditary nephritis
f: Haematuria from childhood, sensorineural deafness and a cone-shaped lens; split, basket-weave basement membrane
h: **Al**port hits **al**l three type IV collagen sites: kidney, ear, eye
x: Usually X-linked, so boys are hit hardest; a transplant can trigger anti-GBM antibodies
m: ACE inhibitors slow the decline; dialysis or transplant later
u: Nephritic syndrome ; Collagen and connective tissue

@ep Berger disease | IgA nephropathy
f: Haematuria starting 1 to 2 days after a cold, mesangial IgA, normal complement
h: **Berger** **b**egins within days
x: Poststreptococcal GN waits 2 to 3 weeks and lowers C3
m: Control BP and protein leak with an ACE inhibitor; IgA vasculitis is the systemic cousin
u: Nephritic syndrome

@ep Kussmaul breathing | Kussmaul respiration
f: Deep, laboured, sighing breaths in severe metabolic acidosis
h: **Kuss**maul **cuss**es the acid out: big breaths blow off CO₂
x: Kussmaul **sign** is a different thing: the JVP rising on inspiration
m: Check glucose, ketones, the anion gap and toxic alcohols
u: Metabolic acidosis and the anion gap ; DKA and HHS

@ep Kussmaul sign | paradoxical JVP rise
f: The neck veins fill more, not less, when the patient breathes in
h: The stiff heart cannot accept the extra blood that inspiration sends, so it backs up into the neck
x: Constrictive pericarditis (and restrictive cardiomyopathy); tamponade gives pulsus paradoxus instead
m: Look for a pericardial knock and a calcified pericardium
u: Pericardial disease ; Cardiomyopathies

@ep Winter formula | expected PaCO₂
f: Expected PaCO₂ = 1.5 × HCO₃⁻ + 8 ± 2 in a metabolic acidosis
h: **One and a half** times the bicarbonate, plus **8**: winter is cold, so you breathe out hard
x: A PaCO₂ outside the range means a second, respiratory disorder
m: Higher than predicted: respiratory acidosis too. Lower: respiratory alkalosis too, as in salicylate poisoning
u: Metabolic acidosis and the anion gap ; Respiratory acid-base disorders and compensation

@ep Trousseau sign | carpopedal spasm
f: The hand clamps shut when a BP cuff stays inflated above systolic for 3 minutes
h: **Trou**sseau's **trou**ser-tight cuff squeezes the hand shut
x: Same surname, different finding: Trousseau syndrome is migratory thrombophlebitis with cancer
m: Check ionised calcium, magnesium, PTH and albumin
u: Calcium, phosphate and magnesium ; Calcium and parathyroid disorders

@ep Trousseau syndrome | migratory thrombophlebitis
f: Tender superficial vein clots that appear in one place, fade, then appear somewhere else
h: **Trou**sseau's **tu**mour throws clots
x: The calcium sign with the same name is the cuff test
m: Hunt for an adenocarcinoma, classically of the pancreas
u: Pancreas ; Tumour markers and paraneoplastic

@ep Chvostek sign | facial nerve tap sign
f: Tapping the facial nerve in front of the ear makes the corner of the mouth twitch
h: **Ch**vostek: **ch**eek tap
x: Positive in some healthy people; Trousseau sign is more specific
m: Low calcium (after thyroid surgery) or low magnesium
u: Calcium, phosphate and magnesium

@ep Beck triad | tamponade triad
f: Low blood pressure, distended neck veins and muffled heart sounds
h: Beck's three Ds: **d**rop in pressure, **d**istended veins, **d**istant heart sounds
x: Pulsus paradoxus travels with it; Kussmaul sign belongs to constriction
m: Pericardiocentesis now
u: Pericardial disease

@ep Dressler syndrome | post-MI autoimmune pericarditis
f: Fever, pleuritic chest pain and a rub weeks after an MI
h: **D**ressler is **d**elayed
x: Pericarditis in the first days after an MI is direct inflammation; Dressler is autoimmune and comes weeks later
m: NSAIDs or aspirin with colchicine
u: MI complications

@ep Eisenmenger syndrome | shunt reversal
f: Late cyanosis and clubbing in someone who had a left-to-right shunt
h: **Eisen**menger: the vessels turn to **iron** (Eisen), stiff with pulmonary hypertension, and the shunt reverses
x: Once established, closing the defect no longer helps
m: Polycythaemia follows; transplant is the definitive treatment
u: Congenital heart disease

@ep Osler nodes | Osler's nodes
f: Painful, raised, red nodules on the pads of the fingers and toes in endocarditis
h: **O**sler = **O**uch
x: Janeway lesions are the painless, flat ones on the palms and soles
m: Blood cultures and echocardiography
u: Endocarditis and rheumatic fever

@ep Janeway lesions | Janeway's lesions
f: Painless flat red spots on the palms and soles in endocarditis
h: **J**aneway: **j**ust painless
x: Septic microemboli, whereas Osler nodes are immune complex lumps that hurt
m: Think acute endocarditis, often Staphylococcus aureus
u: Endocarditis and rheumatic fever

@ep Roth spots | Roth's spots
f: Retinal haemorrhages with pale centres
h: **R**oth spots sit in the **r**etina
x: Also seen in leukaemia and severe anaemia, not only endocarditis
m: Blood cultures and echocardiography if the story fits
u: Endocarditis and rheumatic fever

@ep Libman-Sacks endocarditis | verrucous endocarditis
f: Small sterile vegetations on both surfaces of the mitral valve in lupus
h: SLE causes LSE: **L**ibman-**S**acks **E**ndocarditis
x: Sterile, so blood cultures are negative; often with antiphospholipid antibodies
m: Watch for emboli; anticoagulate if antiphospholipid syndrome is present
u: Endocarditis and rheumatic fever ; Connective tissue diseases

@ep Sheehan syndrome | postpartum pituitary necrosis
f: No breast milk and no periods after a big postpartum haemorrhage
h: **She**ehan: **she** bled after delivery
x: The pituitary grows in pregnancy and outruns its blood supply, so shock infarcts it
m: Replace cortisol first, then thyroxine
u: Pituitary disorders

@ep Waterhouse-Friderichsen syndrome | adrenal apoplexy in sepsis
f: Shock, purpuric rash and bleeding into both adrenal glands
h: **Water**house: a **water**fall of blood into the adrenals
x: Classic with meningococcaemia and DIC
m: Hydrocortisone and antibiotics immediately
u: Adrenal insufficiency and CAH ; Gram-negative cocci

@ep Kallmann syndrome | hypogonadotropic hypogonadism with anosmia
f: Delayed puberty and no sense of smell
h: **Kallmann** cannot smell the **cologne**
x: GnRH neurons fail to migrate with the olfactory neurons, so LH and FSH are low
m: Pulsatile GnRH or sex hormone replacement
u: Sexual differentiation

@ep Mallory-Weiss tear | Mallory-Weiss syndrome
f: Vomiting blood after forceful retching; a superficial tear at the gastro-oesophageal junction
h: **M**allory-**W**eiss is **m**ild: mucosa only
x: Boerhaave is a full-thickness rupture with air in the mediastinum
m: Usually stops by itself
u: Oesophagus

@ep Boerhaave syndrome | oesophageal rupture
f: Chest pain after vomiting, with air in the mediastinum and crackling under the skin of the neck
h: **B**oerhaave: **b**urst
x: Transmural, usually the distal left posterolateral wall; Mallory-Weiss is superficial
m: Surgical emergency
u: Oesophagus

@ep Plummer-Vinson syndrome | Paterson-Brown-Kelly syndrome
f: Difficulty swallowing from oesophageal webs, iron deficiency anaemia and a sore smooth tongue
h: **P**lummer-**V**inson: **p**ale, **v**ery iron-poor, with a web in the throat
x: Raises the risk of squamous cell carcinoma of the oesophagus
m: Replace iron
u: Oesophagus ; Microcytic anaemias

@ep Courvoisier sign | Courvoisier's law
f: Painless jaundice with a palpable, non-tender gallbladder
h: A **calm**, **big** gallbladder means **cancer**
x: Gallstones scar the gallbladder, so it rarely swells
m: Image the head of the pancreas; check CA 19-9
u: Pancreas ; Biliary disease

@ep Virchow node | Virchow's node ; Troisier sign
f: A hard left supraclavicular lymph node
h: Abdominal cancer's **first stop on the left**, where the thoracic duct drains
x: Not Virchow triad (stasis, endothelial injury, hypercoagulability)
m: Look for gastric or other abdominal cancer
u: Tumour markers and paraneoplastic

@ep Virchow triad | Virchow's triad
f: Stasis, endothelial injury and hypercoagulability
h: **SHE** clots: **S**tasis, **H**ypercoagulability, **E**ndothelial damage
x: Same name as the node; the triad is about clots
m: Explains DVT and PE risk after surgery, flights, cancer, pregnancy
u: Pulmonary embolism ; Thrombophilias

@ep Krukenberg tumour | Krukenberg tumor
f: Both ovaries enlarged by mucin-filled signet ring cells
h: The stomach's **signet ring** travels to **both** ovaries
x: A metastasis, usually from gastric cancer, not a primary ovarian tumour
m: Look at the stomach
u: Ovarian tumours

@ep Zollinger-Ellison syndrome | gastrinoma
f: Many ulcers, some beyond the duodenum, with diarrhoea and very high gastrin
h: **Z**ollinger-**E**llison: **z**illions of ulcers
x: Gastrin rises after secretin, when it normally falls; linked to MEN 1
m: High-dose PPI, then find and remove the gastrinoma
u: Gastritis and peptic ulcer ; MEN syndromes

@ep Whipple disease | intestinal lipodystrophy
f: Joint pain, diarrhoea, weight loss and later dementia; PAS-positive foamy macrophages in the gut wall
h: **Whip**ple: **whip**ped, foamy macrophages
x: Caused by Tropheryma whipplei; classic in older white men
m: Antibiotics for months
u: Malabsorption

@ep Charcot triad | cholangitis triad
f: Fever, jaundice and right upper quadrant pain
h: Charcot's three: **hot**, **yellow**, **sore**
x: Add hypotension and confusion for Reynolds pentad; Charcot also named a different triad in MS
m: Ascending cholangitis: antibiotics and ERCP drainage
u: Biliary disease

@ep Budd-Chiari syndrome | hepatic vein thrombosis
f: Painful big liver and ascites from a clot in the hepatic veins
h: **Budd-Chiari** blocks the liver's way **out**
x: No raised JVP, unlike right heart failure; think polycythaemia vera (JAK2), pregnancy, oral contraceptives
m: Anticoagulate and look for the cause
u: Cirrhosis and portal hypertension ; Myeloproliferative neoplasms

@ep Dubin-Johnson syndrome | conjugated hyperbilirubinaemia
f: Mild conjugated jaundice with a black liver
h: **D**ubin-Johnson: **d**ark liver
x: Rotor syndrome looks the same but the liver is not black
m: Benign: reassure
u: Jaundice and bilirubin

@ep Reed-Sternberg cells | owl-eye cells
f: Big cells with two nuclei and prominent nucleoli, CD15 and CD30 positive
h: **Owl eyes** staring out of the lymph node
x: Only a few of them sit in a sea of reactive inflammatory cells
m: Hodgkin lymphoma
u: Lymphomas

@ep Auer rods | Auer bodies
f: Pink needle-shaped inclusions in myeloblasts
h: **A**uer rods: **a**cute myeloid
x: Packed in APL with t(15;17), which can trigger DIC
m: All-trans retinoic acid for APL
u: Leukaemias

@ep Howell-Jolly bodies | nuclear remnants
f: Small dark dots of nuclear material inside red cells
h: **No spleen** left to tidy them away
x: Splenectomy or functional asplenia, as in sickle cell disease
m: Vaccinate against encapsulated bacteria
u: Haemolytic anaemias

@ep Heinz bodies | denatured haemoglobin
f: Clumps of oxidised haemoglobin; bite cells once the spleen plucks them out
h: **H**einz: **h**aemoglobin cooked by oxidants
x: G6PD deficiency after fava beans, primaquine, dapsone or sulfa drugs
m: Avoid the trigger
u: Haemolytic anaemias

@ep Wernicke encephalopathy | Wernicke-Korsakoff
f: Confusion, eye movement palsy and a wide-based gait
h: **COAT**: **c**onfusion, **o**phthalmoplegia, **a**taxia, give **t**hiamine
x: Glucose before thiamine can tip someone into it; Korsakoff adds confabulation and permanent memory loss
m: IV thiamine first
u: Water-soluble vitamins

@ep Brown-Séquard syndrome | cord hemisection
f: Same-side weakness and loss of vibration and position; opposite-side loss of pain and temperature
h: Brown-Séquard **splits** the cord down the middle
x: Pain fibres cross within 1 or 2 segments, so the sensory loss is on the other side
m: Think penetrating trauma or a tumour
u: Spinal cord lesions

@ep Horner syndrome | oculosympathetic palsy
f: Droopy eyelid, small pupil and a dry face on one side
h: **Droopy, tiny, dry**: the sympathetic supply is cut
x: Look for a Pancoast tumour or a carotid dissection
m: Image along the whole sympathetic chain
u: Lung cancer ; Eye: fields and pupils

@ep Argyll Robertson pupil | light-near dissociation
f: Small irregular pupils that constrict to near focus but not to light
h: **ARP**: **a**ccommodation **r**etained, **p**upillary light reflex lost
x: Neurosyphilis (tabes dorsalis); diabetes can do it too
m: Test for syphilis
u: Syphilis ; Eye: fields and pupils

@ep Marcus Gunn pupil | relative afferent pupillary defect
f: Both pupils dilate when the light swings onto the affected eye
h: The swinging light **unmasks** the weak optic nerve
x: Optic neuritis, often the first sign of multiple sclerosis
m: Look for MS
u: Eye: fields and pupils ; Demyelinating disease

@ep Curschmann spirals | Curschmann's spirals
f: Spiral mucus casts of the small airways in sputum
h: **Curs**chmann: **curl**ed spirals
x: Travel with Charcot-Leyden crystals from broken-down eosinophils
m: Asthma
u: Asthma

@ep Pancoast tumour | superior sulcus tumour
f: Apical lung cancer with shoulder pain, hand weakness and Horner syndrome
h: **Pan**coast sits at the **peak** of the lung
x: Compresses the sympathetic chain and the lower brachial plexus (C8, T1)
m: Image the lung apex
u: Lung cancer

@ep Ghon complex | primary tuberculosis complex
f: A calcified lung focus plus a calcified hilar lymph node
h: The **TB** scar has **gone** quiet
x: The Ghon focus alone is just the lung lesion; the complex adds the node
m: Latent TB: think about reactivation risk
u: Mycobacteria

@ep Koplik spots | Koplik's spots
f: Small white spots on red buccal mucosa before the measles rash
h: **K**oplik: **ch**eek spots come first
x: They appear before the rash, so they make the early diagnosis
m: Measles: vitamin A for children
u: RNA viruses

@ep Negri bodies | rabies inclusions
f: Eosinophilic inclusions in the cytoplasm of Purkinje and hippocampal neurons
h: **Negri** bodies are the rabies brain's fingerprints
x: Rabies travels to the brain by retrograde axonal transport
m: Wound care, vaccine and rabies immunoglobulin after exposure
u: RNA viruses

@ep Nikolsky sign | Nikolsky's sign
f: The top layer of skin slides off with gentle rubbing
h: **Nik**olsky: the skin **nicks** off
x: Positive in pemphigus vulgaris and staphylococcal scalded skin; negative in bullous pemphigoid
m: Desmoglein antibodies point to pemphigus
u: Blistering skin disease

@ep Gottron papules | Gottron's papules
f: Violet, scaly bumps over the knuckles
h: **Got**tron's **got** your knuckles
x: With a heliotrope rash and proximal weakness: dermatomyositis
m: Screen for an underlying cancer
u: Connective tissue diseases

@ep Heberden nodes | Heberden's nodes ; Bouchard nodes
f: Bony swellings of the finger joints nearest the nails (DIP)
h: **Heb**erden sits **high** at the DIP; **B**ouchard sits **below** at the PIP
x: Osteoarthritis; rheumatoid arthritis spares the DIP joints
m: Osteoarthritis
u: Arthritis
