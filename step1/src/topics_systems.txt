@ cardio | Cardiovascular | Cardiovascular
# Congenital heart disease | cardio, embryo
! Infant **squats to relieve blue spells**, boot-shaped heart | Tetralogy of Fallot: **anterosuperior** displacement of the infundibular septum
! **Cyanosis at birth** in an infant of a diabetic mother, egg on a string | Transposition: failed **spiralling** of the aorticopulmonary septum
! **Continuous machine-like murmur**, closed by indomethacin | PDA: kept open by **prostaglandin E**
! **Fixed split S2** | Atrial septal defect
! **Leg claudication, rib notching**, arm BP higher than legs, Turner | Coarctation of the aorta
! **Late cyanosis** in a child with a left-to-right shunt | Eisenmenger: pulmonary hypertension reverses the shunt
# Cardiac cycle and murmurs | cardio, physio
! **Crescendo-decrescendo** systolic murmur to the carotids, syncope on exertion | Aortic stenosis: calcified valve or **bicuspid** in the young
! **Holosystolic** blowing murmur at the apex to the axilla | Mitral regurgitation
! **Opening snap then diastolic rumble** | Mitral stenosis: rheumatic fever
! **Mid-systolic click**, murmur later with squatting | Mitral valve prolapse: myxomatous degeneration
! **Early diastolic decrescendo** murmur, bounding pulses | Aortic regurgitation
! Murmur **louder with inspiration** | Right-sided lesion
# Cardiac output and haemodynamics | cardio, physio
! **Fick principle** | Cardiac output = O2 consumption ÷ arteriovenous O2 difference
! **Preload rises** | Stroke volume rises along the Frank-Starling curve
! **Afterload rises** | Stroke volume falls and end-systolic volume rises
# Ischaemic heart disease | cardio, path
! **Chest pain on exertion relieved by rest** | Stable angina: fixed plaque over 70% stenosis
! **Transient ST elevation at rest, early morning**, smoker | Vasospastic angina: treat with CCBs and nitrates
! **ST elevation in II, III, aVF** | Inferior MI: **right coronary artery**
! **ST elevation in V1 to V4** | Anterior MI: **left anterior descending**
# MI complications | cardio, path
! **0 to 24 hours** after MI: sudden death | Ventricular arrhythmia
! **3 to 14 days**: macrophages, granulation tissue, **tamponade** | Free wall rupture
! **Papillary muscle rupture** | Acute mitral regurgitation, often posteromedial with RCA infarct
! **Weeks after MI**: fever, pleuritic pain, rub | Dressler syndrome: autoimmune pericarditis
# Heart failure | cardio
! **Breathless lying flat**, crackles, S3 | Left heart failure
! **Raised JVP, leg oedema**, nutmeg liver | Right heart failure, most often from left heart failure
! Drugs that **improve survival** in HFrEF | ACE inhibitor or ARB or ARNI, beta-blocker, spironolactone, SGLT2 inhibitor
# Cardiomyopathies | cardio
! Young athlete **collapses**, murmur louder with **Valsalva and standing** | Hypertrophic cardiomyopathy: **sarcomere** genes, autosomal dominant
! **Dilated heart** after alcohol, doxorubicin, Chagas or the postpartum period | Dilated cardiomyopathy: systolic failure
! **Stiff heart** with amyloid or haemochromatosis | Restrictive cardiomyopathy
# Pericardial disease | cardio
! **Sharp chest pain better leaning forward**, diffuse ST elevation, PR depression | Acute pericarditis
! **Low BP, raised JVP, muffled heart sounds**, pulsus paradoxus | Cardiac tamponade: **Beck triad**
! **Kussmaul sign**, pericardial knock, calcified pericardium | Constrictive pericarditis
# Endocarditis and rheumatic fever | cardio, micro
! IV drug user with **tricuspid vegetation** | Staphylococcus aureus
! **Dental work**, then subacute endocarditis on a damaged valve | Viridans streptococci: dextran helps them stick
! Endocarditis with **colon cancer** | Streptococcus gallolyticus
! **Joints, carditis, nodules, chorea, rash** after strep throat | Rheumatic fever: **molecular mimicry** with M protein
# Hypertension drugs | cardio, pharm
! **Dry cough and angioedema** | ACE inhibitor: bradykinin builds up
! **Ankle oedema and flushing** | Dihydropyridine CCB
! **Hyperglycaemia, hyperuricaemia, hypercalcaemia** | Thiazide
! **Safe in pregnancy** | Labetalol, hydralazine, methyldopa, nifedipine
# Antiarrhythmics | cardio, pharm
! **Class IC**, contraindicated after MI | Flecainide
! **Pulmonary fibrosis, thyroid change, blue-grey skin** | Amiodarone
! **Cinchonism**, tinnitus | Quinidine
! **Yellow vision**, worse with low potassium | Digoxin toxicity
# Arrhythmias and the ECG | cardio
! **Short PR, delta wave** | Wolff-Parkinson-White: avoid AV-nodal blockers if AF
! **Progressive PR lengthening** then a dropped beat | Mobitz I, Wenckebach: AV node
! **Sudden dropped beats** without PR change | Mobitz II: His-Purkinje, may need a pacemaker
! **Long QT** with congenital deafness | Jervell and Lange-Nielsen
# Vasculitis | cardio, immuno
! Woman over 50 with **jaw claudication** and headache | Giant cell arteritis: risk of blindness, treat with steroids now
! Young Asian woman with **weak arm pulses** | Takayasu arteritis
! **Hepatitis B**, renal and mesenteric aneurysms, spares lungs | Polyarteritis nodosa
! Child with **fever for 5 days, strawberry tongue**, peeling hands | Kawasaki disease: **coronary aneurysms**, give IVIG and aspirin
! **Sinusitis, lung nodules, kidney disease**, c-ANCA | Granulomatosis with polyangiitis
! **Asthma, eosinophilia**, neuropathy, p-ANCA | Eosinophilic granulomatosis with polyangiitis
! Child with **palpable purpura on buttocks**, arthralgia, abdominal pain | IgA vasculitis, Henoch-Schönlein
# Aortic disease | cardio
! **Tearing chest pain to the back**, unequal arm BP | Aortic dissection: hypertension, Marfan
! **Pulsatile abdominal mass** in an older male smoker | Abdominal aortic aneurysm
# Shock | cardio, physio
! **Low CO, high SVR, high PCWP** | Cardiogenic shock
! **Low CO, high SVR, low PCWP** | Hypovolaemic shock
! **High CO, low SVR**, warm skin | Septic shock
# Lipid-lowering drugs | cardio, pharm
! **Myopathy and raised liver enzymes** | Statins: HMG-CoA reductase inhibitors
! **Flushing** prevented by aspirin | Niacin
! **Gallstones** with myopathy alongside statins | Fibrates
# Cardiac tumours | cardio, path
! **Ball-valve** obstruction in the left atrium, tumour plop | Atrial myxoma
! **Rhabdomyoma** in an infant | Tuberous sclerosis

@ endo | Endocrine | Endocrine
# Hypothalamic-pituitary axes | endo, physio
! Problem **in the gland itself** | Primary: the hormone and its stimulator move in opposite directions
! Problem **upstream of the gland** | Secondary: the hormone and its stimulator move together
! **Bitemporal hemianopia** | Pituitary adenoma pressing the optic chiasm
# Pituitary disorders | endo
! **Galactorrhoea and amenorrhoea** | Prolactinoma: treat with cabergoline or bromocriptine
! **Larger hands and shoes, jaw protrusion**, glucose intolerance | Acromegaly: **IGF-1**, then GH not suppressed by glucose
! **Failure to lactate** after a big postpartum haemorrhage | Sheehan syndrome
! **Hypotension and visual loss** suddenly, headache, adenoma | Pituitary apoplexy
# SIADH and diabetes insipidus | endo, renal
! **Low sodium, concentrated urine**, euvolaemic, small cell lung cancer | SIADH
! **Dilute urine**, thirst, improves with desmopressin | Central diabetes insipidus
! Dilute urine **not improving with desmopressin**, lithium use | Nephrogenic diabetes insipidus
# Hyperthyroidism | endo
! **Exophthalmos, pretibial myxoedema**, diffuse goitre | Graves: **TSH receptor stimulating** antibodies
! **Painful tender goitre** after a viral illness, high ESR | Subacute granulomatous thyroiditis, de Quervain
! **Fever, delirium, tachycardia** after surgery in hyperthyroidism | Thyroid storm: beta-blocker, PTU, steroids
! Antithyroid drug for the **first trimester** | Propylthiouracil; methimazole is teratogenic early
# Hypothyroidism | endo
! **Weight gain, cold intolerance**, firm painless goitre, anti-TPO | Hashimoto thyroiditis
! Newborn with **big tongue, umbilical hernia, poor feeding** | Congenital hypothyroidism
# Thyroid cancer | endo, path
! **Psammoma bodies, Orphan Annie nuclei** | Papillary carcinoma: the commonest, good prognosis
! **Calcitonin**, amyloid stroma | Medullary carcinoma: **MEN 2**, RET
! Spread **through the blood**, invades the capsule | Follicular carcinoma
# Calcium and parathyroid disorders | endo
! **Stones, bones, groans, moans**, high Ca, high PTH | Primary hyperparathyroidism: adenoma
! **Low Ca, high PTH** in CKD | Secondary hyperparathyroidism
! **Short 4th and 5th knuckles**, low Ca, high PTH | Pseudohypoparathyroidism: **Gs** defect
! **Perioral tingling, Chvostek sign** after thyroid surgery | Hypocalcaemia from parathyroid damage
# Cushing syndrome | endo
! **High ACTH, cortisol suppressed by high-dose dexamethasone** | Cushing disease: pituitary adenoma
! **High ACTH not suppressed**, small cell lung cancer | Ectopic ACTH
! **Low ACTH** with high cortisol | Adrenal adenoma or exogenous steroids
# Adrenal insufficiency and CAH | endo
! **Hyperpigmentation, hypotension, hyperkalaemia** | Primary adrenal insufficiency, Addison: autoimmune in the US
! **Shock with rash and DIC** in meningococcaemia | Waterhouse-Friderichsen: adrenal haemorrhage
! **Ambiguous genitalia, salt wasting** in a newborn girl | CAH: **21-hydroxylase** deficiency, high 17-hydroxyprogesterone
! CAH with **hypertension and hypokalaemia** | 11-beta-hydroxylase or 17-alpha-hydroxylase deficiency
# Hyperaldosteronism | endo, renal
! **Hypertension, low potassium, low renin** | Primary hyperaldosteronism, Conn: adenoma or bilateral hyperplasia
! **Hypertension, low potassium, high renin** | Secondary: renal artery stenosis
# Adrenal medulla tumours | endo
! **Episodic headache, sweating, palpitations**, high metanephrines | Phaeochromocytoma: **alpha-blocker first**, then beta
! Child with an **abdominal mass crossing the midline**, opsoclonus-myoclonus | Neuroblastoma: **N-myc**, high HVA and VMA
# Diabetes mellitus | endo
! **Insulitis**, HLA-DR3/DR4, autoantibodies | Type 1 diabetes
! **Amyloid in islets**, insulin resistance | Type 2 diabetes
! **Sorbitol** build-up | Cataracts, neuropathy and retinopathy in diabetes
# Diabetes drugs | endo, pharm
! **Lactic acidosis** risk, weight neutral | Metformin
! **Weight gain and hypoglycaemia** | Sulfonylureas: close K-ATP channels
! **Glucosuria, UTIs**, euglycaemic DKA | SGLT2 inhibitors
! **Pancreatitis risk**, weight loss, injection | GLP-1 agonists
# DKA and HHS | endo
! **Kussmaul breathing, fruity breath**, high anion gap | DKA: insulin plus fluids, watch potassium
! Older type 2 diabetic, **glucose over 600, no ketones**, very dehydrated | Hyperosmolar hyperglycaemic state
# MEN syndromes | endo, genetics
! **Pituitary, parathyroid, pancreas** | MEN 1: menin
! **Medullary thyroid, phaeochromocytoma, parathyroid** | MEN 2A: RET
! **Medullary thyroid, phaeochromocytoma, mucosal neuromas**, Marfanoid | MEN 2B: RET
# Carcinoid syndrome | endo, gi
! **Flushing, diarrhoea, right-sided valve disease** | Carcinoid syndrome: needs **liver metastases**, high 5-HIAA
! Carcinoid tumour **in the appendix** with no flushing | The liver clears serotonin until metastases bypass it
! **Pellagra** in carcinoid syndrome | Tryptophan is diverted into serotonin, so niacin runs short

@ gi | Gastrointestinal | Gastrointestinal
# GI embryology | gi, embryo
! **Projectile non-bilious vomiting** at 3 weeks, olive mass | Hypertrophic pyloric stenosis: hypochloraemic metabolic alkalosis
! **Bilious vomiting, double bubble**, Down syndrome | Duodenal atresia
! **Painless rectal bleeding** in a 2 year old, ectopic gastric tissue | Meckel diverticulum
! **Delayed meconium**, empty rectal vault, explosive stool | Hirschsprung disease: no **neural crest** migration, RET
! **Bilious vomiting** in a newborn with volvulus | Malrotation
# GI secretions and hormones | gi, physio
! Hormone that **contracts the gallbladder** | CCK, from I cells
! Hormone that releases **pancreatic bicarbonate** | Secretin, from S cells
! **Gastrin** rises with | Proton pump inhibitors, achlorhydria, gastrinoma
# Oesophagus | gi
! **Dysphagia to solids and liquids**, bird beak | Achalasia: loss of **myenteric** NO and VIP neurons
! **Columnar metaplasia** after long reflux | Barrett oesophagus: risk of adenocarcinoma
! **Vomiting blood after retching**, superficial tear | Mallory-Weiss
! **Full-thickness rupture** after vomiting, mediastinal air | Boerhaave
! **Iron deficiency, oesophageal webs**, glossitis | Plummer-Vinson
# Gastritis and peptic ulcer | gi
! **Pain better with food** | Duodenal ulcer: H. pylori
! **Pain worse with food** | Gastric ulcer: NSAIDs, H. pylori
! **Multiple jejunal ulcers**, high gastrin | Zollinger-Ellison syndrome
# Malabsorption | gi
! **Itchy blisters on elbows**, iron deficiency, diarrhoea | Coeliac disease: anti-tTG IgA, villous atrophy
! **Arthralgia, diarrhoea, dementia**, PAS-positive macrophages | Whipple disease: Tropheryma whipplei
# IBD | gi
! **Skip lesions, transmural**, fistulas, cobblestone mucosa | Crohn disease: non-caseating granulomas
! **Continuous from the rectum**, bloody diarrhoea, pseudopolyps | Ulcerative colitis: PSC, toxic megacolon
# Colorectal neoplasia | gi, path
! **Thousands of polyps** by the teens | FAP: **APC** gene
! Polyps with **osteomas and dental abnormalities** | Gardner syndrome
! **Hamartomatous polyps with lip pigmentation** | Peutz-Jeghers
! **Iron deficiency anaemia** in an older man | Right-sided colon cancer until proven otherwise
# Diverticular and vascular bowel disease | gi
! **Left lower quadrant pain** and fever in an older adult | Diverticulitis
! **Pain out of proportion** to examination, AF | Acute mesenteric ischaemia
! **Splenic flexure** ischaemia after hypotension | Watershed area
# Jaundice and bilirubin | gi
! **Mild unconjugated bilirubin rise with stress or fasting** | Gilbert syndrome: low UGT activity
! **Black liver**, conjugated hyperbilirubinaemia | Dubin-Johnson
! **Severe unconjugated jaundice** in a newborn, kernicterus | Crigler-Najjar type I
# Viral hepatitis | gi, micro
! **HBsAg positive, anti-HBc IgM positive** | Acute hepatitis B
! **Anti-HBs only** | Vaccinated
! **Anti-HBc positive and anti-HBs positive** | Past infection, now immune
! Only **anti-HBc positive** | Window period
! Hepatitis needing **HBV coinfection** | Hepatitis D
# Cirrhosis and portal hypertension | gi
! **Caput medusae, varices, ascites**, spider angiomas | Portal hypertension in cirrhosis
! **Asterixis and confusion** in cirrhosis | Hepatic encephalopathy: lactulose, rifaximin
! Cirrhosis with **ascites and fever** | Spontaneous bacterial peritonitis: neutrophils over 250 per microlitre
# Alcohol and fatty liver | gi
! **AST to ALT ratio over 2**, Mallory bodies | Alcoholic hepatitis
! **ALT higher than AST**, obesity, insulin resistance | Non-alcoholic fatty liver disease
# Metal overload | gi, genetics
! **Bronze skin, diabetes**, cirrhosis, high ferritin | Haemochromatosis: **HFE** C282Y
! **Kayser-Fleischer rings**, tremor, low ceruloplasmin | Wilson disease: **ATP7B**
# Biliary disease | gi
! **Fat, female, forty**, RUQ pain after fatty meals | Gallstones: cholesterol stones
! **Fever, jaundice, RUQ pain** | Ascending cholangitis: Charcot triad
! **Middle-aged woman, itch, antimitochondrial antibodies** | Primary biliary cholangitis
! **Beading of bile ducts**, ulcerative colitis, p-ANCA | Primary sclerosing cholangitis
# Pancreas | gi
! **Epigastric pain to the back**, high lipase | Acute pancreatitis: gallstones or alcohol
! **Painless jaundice with a palpable gallbladder** | Pancreatic head cancer: Courvoisier sign, CA 19-9
! **Migratory thrombophlebitis** | Trousseau syndrome: pancreatic cancer
# GI drugs | gi, pharm
! **Hypomagnesaemia, C. difficile, fractures** with long use | Proton pump inhibitors
! **Gynaecomastia** and CYP inhibition | Cimetidine
! Antiemetic blocking **5-HT3** | Ondansetron: QT prolongation
! Antiemetic and prokinetic blocking **D2** | Metoclopramide: dystonia

@ heme | Haematology & oncology | Hematology and oncology
# Microcytic anaemias | heme
! **Low ferritin, high TIBC** | Iron deficiency anaemia
! **Normal ferritin, high RBC count**, target cells | Thalassaemia trait
! **Basophilic stippling**, abdominal pain, wrist drop | Lead poisoning: inhibits **ALA dehydratase and ferrochelatase**
! **Ringed sideroblasts** with high iron | Sideroblastic anaemia: ALA synthase, alcohol, isoniazid needs B6
# Macrocytic anaemias | heme
! **Hypersegmented neutrophils** with neuropathy | B12 deficiency: pernicious anaemia, anti-intrinsic factor
! Macrocytic anaemia **without neuropathy** after methotrexate | Folate deficiency
# Haemolytic anaemias | heme
! **Spherocytes, negative Coombs**, family history | Hereditary spherocytosis: **ankyrin or spectrin**, high MCHC
! **Bite cells, Heinz bodies** after fava beans or primaquine | G6PD deficiency: X-linked
! **Painful crises, dactylitis**, autosplenectomy | Sickle cell disease: **glutamate to valine** in beta-globin
! **Dark morning urine**, pancytopenia, venous thrombosis | PNH: **CD55 and CD59** lost, complement lysis
! **Warm IgG** haemolysis with SLE or CLL | Warm autoimmune haemolytic anaemia
! **Cold IgM** haemolysis with Mycoplasma or EBV | Cold agglutinin disease
# Marrow failure | heme
! **Pancytopenia with a fatty marrow** after chloramphenicol or parvovirus | Aplastic anaemia
! **Low iron, high ferritin** with inflammation | Anaemia of chronic disease: hepcidin
# Platelet disorders | heme
! **Isolated low platelets**, petechiae, after a virus | Immune thrombocytopenia
! **Fever, confusion, kidney injury, schistocytes, low platelets** | TTP: **ADAMTS13** deficiency, plasma exchange
! **Giant platelets**, no aggregation to ristocetin | Bernard-Soulier: **GPIb**
! **No aggregation**, normal size | Glanzmann: **GPIIb/IIIa**
! Platelets fall **5 to 10 days after heparin**, clots | Heparin-induced thrombocytopenia: antibodies to **PF4**-heparin
# Coagulation disorders | heme
! Boy with **haemarthrosis**, prolonged PTT, normal PT | Haemophilia A: factor VIII
! **Mucosal bleeding**, heavy periods, prolonged bleeding time | Von Willebrand disease: the commonest inherited
! **Bleeding and clotting at once**, schistocytes, low fibrinogen | DIC
# Thrombophilias | heme
! **Activated protein C resistance** | Factor V Leiden: the commonest inherited
! **Skin necrosis** starting warfarin | Protein C deficiency
! **Miscarriages, clots, prolonged PTT** that does not correct | Antiphospholipid syndrome
# Anticoagulants | heme, pharm
! Heparin reversal | Protamine sulfate
! Warfarin reversal, urgent | Prothrombin complex concentrate plus vitamin K
! **Dabigatran** reversal | Idarucizumab
! **Factor Xa inhibitor** reversal | Andexanet alfa
# Leukaemias | heme, path
! Child with **bone pain, TdT-positive** blasts | ALL: good prognosis with t(12;21)
! **Auer rods**, DIC | AML M3, APL: **t(15;17)**, all-trans retinoic acid
! Older adult with **smudge cells** | CLL: CD5 positive B cells
! **Very high white count**, big spleen, low LAP | CML: t(9;22), imatinib
! **TRAP-positive** cells with hair-like projections | Hairy cell leukaemia: dry tap
# Lymphomas | heme, path
! **Owl-eye Reed-Sternberg** cells, contiguous spread, B symptoms | Hodgkin lymphoma: CD15 and CD30
! **Starry sky**, jaw mass in Africa | Burkitt lymphoma: **t(8;14)**, EBV
! **Waxing and waning** painless nodes in an older adult | Follicular lymphoma: t(14;18)
! **Mycosis fungoides**: skin T-cell lymphoma with Pautrier microabscesses | Cutaneous T-cell lymphoma
# Plasma cell disorders | heme
! **Bone pain, high calcium, kidney injury, anaemia**, rouleaux | Multiple myeloma: **M spike**, Bence Jones protein
! **IgM M spike**, hyperviscosity, blurred vision | Waldenström macroglobulinaemia
# Myeloproliferative neoplasms | heme
! **Itch after a hot shower**, high haematocrit, low EPO | Polycythaemia vera: **JAK2**
! **Teardrop cells**, dry tap, huge spleen | Primary myelofibrosis
# Langerhans cell histiocytosis | heme
! Child with **lytic skull lesions**, rash, **tennis racket granules** | Langerhans cell histiocytosis: CD1a and S100
! **Diabetes insipidus, lytic skull lesions, bulging eyes** | Hand-Schüller-Christian triad of LCH
# Chemotherapy toxicities | heme, pharm
! **Cardiotoxicity** | Doxorubicin: give dexrazoxane
! **Pulmonary fibrosis** | Bleomycin, busulfan
! **Haemorrhagic cystitis** | Cyclophosphamide: give mesna
! **Peripheral neuropathy** | Vincristine
! **Nephrotoxicity and ototoxicity** | Cisplatin: amifostine

# Connective tissue diseases | msk, immuno

# Neuromuscular junction | msk, neuro
! **Weakness worse at the end of the day**, ptosis | Myasthenia gravis: antibodies to **postsynaptic ACh receptors**, thymoma

# Blistering skin disease | msk, immuno

@ neuro | Neurology & special senses | Neurology and special senses
