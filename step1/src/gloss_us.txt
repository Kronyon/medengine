# medengine-STEP1 US abbreviations and terms with the UK equivalent. ABBR | meaning
ED | Emergency department (UK: A&E)
ER | Emergency room (UK: A&E)
ICU | Intensive care unit (UK: ITU or ICU)
OR | Operating room (UK: theatre)
PCP | Primary care physician (UK: GP); also Pneumocystis pneumonia
NP | Nurse practitioner (UK: advanced nurse practitioner)
PA | Physician assistant (UK: physician associate)
EKG | Electrocardiogram (UK: ECG)
CBC | Complete blood count (UK: FBC)
BMP | Basic metabolic panel: Na⁺, K⁺, Cl⁻, HCO₃⁻, BUN, creatinine, glucose (UK: U&E plus glucose)
CMP | Comprehensive metabolic panel: BMP plus liver tests, protein and calcium (UK: U&E, LFTs and bone profile)
BUN | Blood urea nitrogen in mg/dL (UK: urea in mmol/L, BUN × 0.357)
UA | Urinalysis (UK: urine dipstick and microscopy)
PPD | Purified protein derivative skin test for TB (UK: Mantoux test)
CVA | Cerebrovascular accident, a stroke; also costovertebral angle
CVA tenderness | Costovertebral angle tenderness (UK: renal angle tenderness)
HPI | History of present illness (UK: history of presenting complaint)
ROS | Review of systems (UK: systems review)
s/p | Status post: after a procedure or event
WNL | Within normal limits
NPO | Nothing by mouth (UK: NBM)
q.d. | Once daily (UK: od)
b.i.d. | Twice daily (UK: bd)
t.i.d. | Three times daily (UK: tds)
q.i.d. | Four times daily (UK: qds)
PRN | As needed (UK: prn)
OTC | Over the counter
GERD | Gastroesophageal reflux disease (UK: GORD)
OCP | Oral contraceptive pill (UK: COCP)
STD | Sexually transmitted disease (UK: STI)
IVDU | Intravenous drug use (UK: people who inject drugs)
EtOH | Alcohol (ethanol)
DNR | Do not resuscitate (UK: DNACPR)
POA | Power of attorney (UK: lasting power of attorney)
Medicare | US federal insurance for people 65 and over and some with disabilities
Medicaid | US state and federal insurance for people on low incomes
Tdap | Tetanus, diphtheria and acellular pertussis booster
Acetaminophen | Paracetamol (UK)
Albuterol | Salbutamol (UK)
Epinephrine | Adrenaline (UK)
Norepinephrine | Noradrenaline (UK)
Isoproterenol | Isoprenaline (UK)
Meperidine | Pethidine (UK)
Glyburide | Glibenclamide (UK)
Rifampin | Rifampicin (UK)
Cyclosporine | Ciclosporin (UK)
Hydrochlorothiazide | The US go-to thiazide (UK: bendroflumethiazide or indapamide)
Lidocaine | Lidocaine (UK name too; formerly lignocaine)
Tylenol | Brand of acetaminophen (paracetamol)
Coumadin | Brand of warfarin
Lasix | Brand of furosemide
mEq/L | Milliequivalents per liter: equal to mmol/L for Na⁺, K⁺, Cl⁻ and HCO₃⁻
mg/dL | Milligrams per deciliter: the US mass unit (UK uses mmol/L or µmol/L)
°F | Fahrenheit: (°F − 32) × 5/9 = °C; 100.4°F is 38°C
lb | Pound: kg = lb ÷ 2.2
in | Inch: 2.54 cm
mm Hg | Millimeters of mercury (UK gases read in kPa: ÷ 7.5)
