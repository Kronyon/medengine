@ biochem | Biochemistry & genetics | Biochemistry
# DNA repair defects | biochem, genetics
! Child with **freckling and blistering after minutes of sun**, skin cancers by age 10 | Xeroderma pigmentosum: **nucleotide excision repair** of thymine dimers fails
! **Colon cancer before 50** without many polyps, plus endometrial cancer in relatives | Lynch syndrome: **mismatch repair** genes, microsatellite instability
! Toddler with **wobbly gait, spider veins on the eyes** and repeated sinus infections | Ataxia-telangiectasia: **ATM** fails to repair double-strand breaks, low IgA
! Woman with **breast and ovarian cancer** in young relatives | BRCA1 or BRCA2: **homologous recombination** of double-strand breaks
# Inheritance and imprinting | genetics
! Same 15q deletion, **father's copy lost**: hyperphagia, obesity, hypotonia | Prader-Willi: paternal deletion or **maternal uniparental disomy**
! Same 15q deletion, **mother's copy lost**: happy puppet, seizures, ataxia | Angelman: **UBE3A** from mother missing
! Disease **worse and earlier each generation** | Anticipation: **trinucleotide repeat expansion**, e.g. Huntington, myotonic dystrophy, fragile X
! Affected mother passes it to **all children**, affected father to **none**, variable severity | Mitochondrial inheritance with **heteroplasmy**
# Trisomies | genetics
! **Flat face, single palmar crease, duodenal atresia**, endocardial cushion defect | Down syndrome: usually **meiotic nondisjunction**, raised risk with maternal age
! **Clenched fists with overlapping fingers, rocker-bottom feet**, small jaw | Edwards syndrome, trisomy 18
! **Cleft lip, holoprosencephaly, polydactyly**, scalp defect | Patau syndrome, trisomy 13
! Quad screen: **low AFP and oestriol, high hCG and inhibin A** | Down syndrome pattern
# Sex chromosome disorders | genetics, repro
! **Tall, small firm testes, gynaecomastia**, infertile man | Klinefelter 47,XXY: **Barr body**, high FSH and LH, low testosterone
! **Short girl, webbed neck, coarctation**, streak ovaries, horseshoe kidney | Turner 45,X: **primary ovarian failure**, high FSH
# Collagen and connective tissue | biochem, msk
! **Blue sclerae, multiple fractures** from minor falls, hearing loss | Osteogenesis imperfecta: **type I collagen** defect
! **Hypermobile joints, stretchy skin**, easy bruising, berry aneurysms | Ehlers-Danlos: collagen processing, often **type III or V**
! Boy with **kinky brittle hair**, hypotonia, growth failure | Menkes: **ATP7A**, low copper, so lysyl oxidase fails
! **Bleeding gums, corkscrew hairs**, poor wound healing on a tea and toast diet | Scurvy: no **vitamin C**, so proline and lysine are not hydroxylated
# Marfan syndrome | genetics, cardio
! **Tall, long fingers, upward lens dislocation**, aortic root dilatation | Marfan: **fibrillin-1**, autosomal dominant
! Marfan-like body but **lens dislocates downward**, intellectual disability, clots | Homocystinuria: **cystathionine synthase** deficiency
# Glycogen storage diseases | biochem
! Infant with **severe fasting hypoglycaemia, big liver**, lactic acidosis, high urate and lipids | Von Gierke: **glucose-6-phosphatase** deficiency
! Floppy infant with a **huge heart** and early death | Pompe: lysosomal **acid alpha-glucosidase** deficiency
! **Muscle cramps on exercise, myoglobinuria**, no lactate rise, second wind | McArdle: **muscle glycogen phosphorylase** deficiency
! Like von Gierke but **milder, normal lactate**, abnormal glycogen with short branches | Cori: **debranching enzyme** deficiency
# Lysosomal storage diseases | biochem
! **Cherry-red macula, no hepatosplenomegaly**, Ashkenazi infant, onion-skin lysosomes | Tay-Sachs: **hexosaminidase A** deficiency
! **Cherry-red macula with hepatosplenomegaly**, foam cells | Niemann-Pick: **sphingomyelinase** deficiency
! **Crumpled tissue paper macrophages**, bone crises, big spleen | Gaucher: **glucocerebrosidase** deficiency, the commonest
! Young man with **burning hands and feet, angiokeratomas**, early kidney and heart disease, X-linked | Fabry: **alpha-galactosidase A** deficiency
! **Coarse face, corneal clouding**, airway obstruction | Hurler: **alpha-L-iduronidase** deficiency
! Hurler-like boy with **clear corneas and aggressive behaviour**, X-linked | Hunter: **iduronate-2-sulfatase** deficiency
# Amino acid disorders | biochem
! Infant with **musty odour, fair skin**, intellectual disability | PKU: **phenylalanine hydroxylase** or BH4 deficiency, tyrosine becomes essential
! **Urine turns black on standing**, dark ear cartilage, arthritis | Alkaptonuria: **homogentisate oxidase** deficiency
! Newborn with **sweet-smelling urine** and poor feeding | Maple syrup urine disease: **branched-chain alpha-ketoacid dehydrogenase**, needs thiamine
! **Hexagonal crystals** and recurrent stones, positive cyanide-nitroprusside | Cystinuria: **COLA** transporter defect in the PCT
# Urea cycle and hyperammonaemia | biochem
! Newborn boy with **vomiting, lethargy, high ammonia**, high orotic acid, **low BUN** | Ornithine transcarbamylase deficiency, X-linked
! **High orotic acid without high ammonia**, megaloblastic anaemia unhelped by B12 | Orotic aciduria: **UMP synthase**, treat with uridine
# Fructose and galactose disorders | biochem
! Baby **vomits and becomes hypoglycaemic after starting fruit juice** | Hereditary fructose intolerance: **aldolase B**
! Infant with **cataracts, jaundice and E. coli sepsis** after milk | Classic galactosaemia: **GALT** deficiency
! **Cataracts only**, otherwise well | Galactokinase deficiency
# Water-soluble vitamins | biochem
! Alcoholic with **confusion, ataxia, eye movement palsy** | Wernicke: **thiamine B1**, give before glucose
! **Diarrhoea, dermatitis on sun-exposed skin, dementia** | Pellagra: **niacin B3**, also Hartnup and carcinoid
! **Macrocytic anaemia with neuropathy** and dorsal column loss | **Vitamin B12**: high methylmalonic acid and homocysteine
! **Macrocytic anaemia without neuropathy**, normal methylmalonic acid | **Folate**: high homocysteine only
# Fat-soluble vitamins | biochem
! **Night blindness, dry eyes**, Bitot spots | Vitamin A deficiency
! **Haemolysis and ataxia** with posterior column signs, normal B12 | Vitamin E deficiency
! Newborn **bleeding on day 2 to 7** without prophylaxis | Vitamin K deficiency: sterile gut, low placental transfer
# Lipid transport and dyslipidaemias | biochem, cardio
! **Very high LDL, tendon xanthomas**, MI in young adults | Familial hypercholesterolaemia: **LDL receptor** defect
! **Milky plasma, eruptive xanthomas, pancreatitis** in a child | Type I: **lipoprotein lipase** or apoC-II deficiency
! **Tuberoeruptive xanthomas, palmar creases** yellow | Dysbetalipoproteinaemia: **apoE2**
# Purine and pyrimidine disorders | biochem
! Boy with **self-mutilation, gout, dystonia** | Lesch-Nyhan: **HGPRT** deficiency, X-linked
! Infant with **SCID and no T or B cells**, toxic dATP | **Adenosine deaminase** deficiency
# Fatty acid oxidation | biochem
! Child **hypoglycaemic after fasting with low ketones**, may die after illness | **MCAD** deficiency: fasting hypoketotic hypoglycaemia
! **Muscle weakness and myoglobinuria** on exercise, low ketones, high fatty acids | **Carnitine** deficiency: long-chain fatty acids cannot enter mitochondria
# Pyruvate and glycolysis defects | biochem, heme
! Neonate with **lactic acidosis, high alanine** and neurological defects | Pyruvate dehydrogenase deficiency: treat with **ketogenic diet** of lysine and leucine
! **Haemolysis with no spherocytes**, high 2,3-BPG | **Pyruvate kinase** deficiency
# Mitochondrial disorders | genetics, neuro
! **Stroke-like episodes, lactic acidosis**, ragged red fibres | MELAS: mitochondrial inheritance
! Young man with **sudden painless bilateral vision loss** and maternal relatives affected | Leber hereditary optic neuropathy
# Lab techniques | biochem
! Test to **detect a protein** with a labelled antibody after gel separation | Western blot
! Test to **find DNA** sequences | Southern blot; **RNA** is Northern
! **Amplify DNA** from tiny amounts | PCR

@ immuno | Immunology | Immunology
# MHC and T cells | immuno
! **CD8 T cells** recognise antigen on | **MHC I** on every nucleated cell, with beta-2 microglobulin
! **CD4 T cells** recognise antigen on | **MHC II** on antigen-presenting cells, with the invariant chain
! **HLA-B27** link | Ankylosing spondylitis, reactive arthritis, psoriatic and IBD arthritis
! **HLA-DR3 and DR4** link | Type 1 diabetes; DR4 with rheumatoid arthritis
# Hypersensitivity types | immuno
! **Minutes after a bee sting**: wheeze, urticaria, hypotension | Type I: **IgE** cross-linked on mast cells
! **Antibody against a cell or tissue**: Goodpasture, Graves, autoimmune haemolysis | Type II: cytotoxic or antibody-mediated
! **Immune complexes** deposit: serum sickness, PSGN, SLE nephritis | Type III: **low complement**
! **48 to 72 hours** after contact: nickel rash, positive PPD | Type IV: **T-cell** mediated, no antibody
# B-cell immunodeficiencies | immuno
! Boy over **6 months** with recurrent bacterial infections, **absent B cells** and tonsils | Bruton X-linked agammaglobulinaemia: **BTK**
! **Anaphylaxis to a blood transfusion**, frequent sinus infections, giardiasis | **Selective IgA deficiency**
! Adult with **normal B-cell numbers** but low immunoglobulins, autoimmunity | Common variable immunodeficiency
# T-cell and combined immunodeficiencies | immuno
! Newborn with **tetany from low calcium, absent thymic shadow**, conotruncal defect | DiGeorge: **22q11** deletion, third and fourth pouches
! Infant with **thrush, chronic diarrhoea, no thymic shadow**, severe infections of all kinds | SCID: often **IL-2 receptor gamma chain**, X-linked
! Boy with **eczema, thrombocytopenia, small platelets**, recurrent infections | Wiskott-Aldrich: **WAS** gene
! **High IgM, very low IgG, IgA, IgE**, Pneumocystis | Hyper-IgM: **CD40 ligand** defect, no class switching
# Phagocyte and complement defects | immuno
! **Catalase-positive** infections, Staph aureus and Aspergillus; nitroblue tetrazolium negative | Chronic granulomatous disease: **NADPH oxidase**
! **Delayed umbilical cord separation**, no pus | Leukocyte adhesion deficiency: **CD18** integrin
! **Giant granules** in neutrophils, albinism, peripheral neuropathy | Chédiak-Higashi: **LYST**, microtubule trafficking
! **Recurrent Neisseria** infections | Terminal complement **C5 to C9** deficiency
! **Swelling of face and gut without urticaria**, worse with ACE inhibitors | Hereditary angioedema: **C1 esterase inhibitor** deficiency, low C4
# Transplant rejection | immuno
! Kidney turns **blue within minutes** of reperfusion | Hyperacute: **preformed antibodies**
! **Weeks to months**: lymphocytic infiltrate | Acute rejection: **T cells** against donor MHC
! **Months to years**: vessel wall thickening, fibrosis | Chronic rejection
! **Rash, jaundice, diarrhoea** after bone marrow transplant | Graft-versus-host disease: **donor T cells**
# Immunosuppressants | immuno, pharm
! **Gum hyperplasia, hirsutism**, nephrotoxicity | Cyclosporine: **calcineurin** inhibitor
! Calcineurin inhibitor with **more diabetes and neurotoxicity**, no hirsutism | Tacrolimus
! Inhibits **mTOR**, not nephrotoxic, causes hyperlipidaemia | Sirolimus
! Check **TPMT** before starting | Azathioprine: toxic with **allopurinol**
# Transfusion reactions | immuno, heme
! **Fever, chills, back pain, red urine** within an hour | Acute haemolytic: **ABO** mismatch, type II
! **Breathless within 6 hours**, bilateral infiltrates, normal heart | TRALI: donor antibodies activate neutrophils
! **Fever and chills only** | Febrile non-haemolytic: **cytokines** from donor white cells
# Autoantibodies | immuno
! **Anti-dsDNA and anti-Smith** | SLE, dsDNA tracks nephritis
! **Anti-histone** | Drug-induced lupus: hydralazine, procainamide, isoniazid
! **Anti-centromere** | Limited scleroderma, CREST
! **Anti-Scl-70** | Diffuse scleroderma
! **Anti-Jo-1** | Polymyositis or dermatomyositis
# Vaccines | immuno, micro
! **Live attenuated** vaccine | Strong cellular immunity; avoid in pregnancy and immunodeficiency
! **Toxoid** vaccines | Tetanus and diphtheria

@ path | General pathology | Pathology
# Cell injury | path
! **Cell swelling, fatty change, ribosome detachment**, still fixable | Reversible injury from ATP loss
! **Membrane damage, nuclear pyknosis**, mitochondrial calcium | Irreversible injury
# Necrosis types | path
! **Ghost outlines** of cells after infarction | Coagulative necrosis, solid organs except brain
! **Brain infarct** or abscess turns liquid | Liquefactive necrosis
! **Cheese-like** centre of a granuloma | Caseous necrosis, TB and fungi
! **Chalky white** areas in the pancreas, calcium soaps | Fat necrosis, saponification
! **Vessel wall** pink and smudgy in malignant hypertension | Fibrinoid necrosis
# Apoptosis | path
! **Cytochrome c** release, BAX and BAK | Intrinsic pathway, held back by **BCL-2**
! **Fas ligand** or TNF triggers | Extrinsic pathway
! **Eosinophilic cytoplasm, pyknosis**, no inflammation | Apoptosis, not necrosis
# Acute inflammation | path
! **Leukocyte rolling** on endothelium | **Selectins**
! **Tight adhesion** of neutrophils | **Integrins** binding ICAM-1
! Chemotaxis signals | **C5a, IL-8, LTB4**, kallikrein
# Chronic inflammation and granulomas | path
! **Epithelioid macrophages and giant cells** | Granuloma: IFN-gamma from Th1 cells activates macrophages
! **Non-caseating granulomas** with hilar nodes and high ACE | Sarcoidosis
# Wound healing and scars | path
! **Raised scar staying within the wound** | Hypertrophic scar: type III collagen
! **Scar growing beyond the wound**, earlobes | Keloid: type I and III collagen
# Amyloidosis | path
! **Apple-green birefringence** with Congo red | Amyloid: beta-pleated sheet
! Amyloid from **light chains** in myeloma | AL amyloidosis
! Amyloid in **long-term dialysis** | Beta-2 microglobulin
# Neoplasia genes | path, genetics
! **Oncogene**, one hit is enough | Gain of function, e.g. **KRAS, MYC, HER2**
! **Tumour suppressor**, two hits needed | Loss of function, e.g. **TP53, RB**
! **Philadelphia chromosome** t(9;22) | BCR-ABL in CML, tyrosine kinase
! **t(8;14)** with a starry sky | Burkitt lymphoma: **c-MYC**
! **t(14;18)** | Follicular lymphoma: **BCL-2** overexpression
# Tumour markers and paraneoplastic | path
! **AFP** high | Hepatocellular carcinoma, yolk sac tumour
! **CA-125** high | Epithelial ovarian cancer
! **Hypercalcaemia with low PTH**, squamous lung cancer | **PTHrP**
! **Polycythaemia** with a kidney mass | Renal cell carcinoma making EPO
# Carcinogens | path
! **Aniline dyes** at work | Bladder transitional cell carcinoma
! **Asbestos** | Mesothelioma and, more often, bronchogenic carcinoma
! **Aflatoxin** | Hepatocellular carcinoma
! **Vinyl chloride** | Liver angiosarcoma
# Calcification | path
! Calcium deposits in **damaged tissue with normal serum calcium** | Dystrophic calcification
! Calcium in **normal tissue with high calcium or phosphate** | Metastatic calcification
# Cell adaptations | path
! **Columnar cells in the lower oesophagus** after reflux | Metaplasia, Barrett
! **Disordered growth** that can still regress | Dysplasia

@ pharm | General pharmacology | Pharmacology
# Pharmacokinetics | pharm
! **Volume of distribution** formula | **Dose divided by plasma concentration**
! **Loading dose** formula | Target concentration × Vd ÷ bioavailability
! **Steady state** is reached in | **4 to 5 half-lives**, whatever the dose
# Elimination kinetics | pharm
! **Constant amount** eliminated per hour | **Zero-order**: ethanol, phenytoin, aspirin at high dose
! **Constant fraction** eliminated per hour | First-order
# CYP450 interactions | pharm
! **Inducers**: drug levels fall | Carbamazepine, rifampin, phenytoin, St John's wort, chronic alcohol
! **Inhibitors**: drug levels rise | Macrolides, azoles, grapefruit, cimetidine, ritonavir, acute alcohol
# Receptor pharmacology | pharm
! Competitive antagonist **shifts the curve right**, same maximum | Potency falls, efficacy unchanged
! Non-competitive antagonist **lowers the maximum** | Efficacy falls
! **Partial agonist** given with a full agonist | Acts as an antagonist: e.g. buprenorphine precipitates withdrawal
# Therapeutic index | pharm
! **TD50 divided by ED50** | Therapeutic index: narrow for warfarin, digoxin, lithium, theophylline
! **Narrow therapeutic index** drugs | Warfarin, digoxin, lithium, phenytoin, theophylline, aminoglycosides: check levels
# Autonomic receptors | pharm
! **Gq**: smooth muscle contraction | Alpha-1, M1, M3, H1, V1
! **Gs**: cAMP up | Beta-1, beta-2, D1, H2, V2
! **Gi**: cAMP down | Alpha-2, M2, D2
# Cholinergic drugs and toxicity | pharm
! **Diarrhoea, urination, miosis, bronchospasm, bradycardia**, sweating, farmer | Organophosphate poisoning: give **atropine and pralidoxime**
! **Hot, dry, red, blind, mad**, urinary retention | Anticholinergic toxicity: physostigmine
# Adrenergic drugs | pharm
! **BP up, reflex bradycardia** | Phenylephrine, a pure alpha-1 agonist
! **HR up, BP down**, beta agonist only | Isoproterenol
! Epinephrine after an **alpha blocker** | BP falls: epinephrine reversal
# Toxicology and antidotes | pharm
! **Acetaminophen** overdose | **N-acetylcysteine** replenishes glutathione
! **Cherry-red skin**, headache in a house fire | Carbon monoxide: 100% or hyperbaric oxygen
! **Bitter almond breath**, lactic acidosis after a house fire | Cyanide: hydroxocobalamin
! **Chocolate-brown blood** after dapsone or a topical anaesthetic | Methaemoglobinaemia: methylene blue
! **Wide QRS** after an antidepressant overdose | TCA toxicity: sodium bicarbonate
# Drug reactions | pharm
! **Fever, rash, eosinophilia, organ involvement** weeks after a new drug | DRESS
! **Mucosal blistering and skin sloughing** after sulfonamides or lamotrigine | Stevens-Johnson or TEN
! **Flushing and vomiting** with alcohol | Disulfiram-like: metronidazole, some cephalosporins, sulfonylureas
# Teratogens | pharm, repro
! **Phocomelia** | Thalidomide
! **Neural tube defects** | Valproate, carbamazepine
! **Ebstein anomaly** | Lithium
! **Nasal hypoplasia**, stippled epiphyses | Warfarin
! **Renal damage** in the fetus | ACE inhibitors

@ pubhealth | Biostatistics, epidemiology & ethics | Public health sciences
# Test performance | biostats
! **Ruling out** a disease | Highly **sensitive** test: SnNOut
! **Ruling in** a disease | Highly **specific** test: SpPIn
! Prevalence rises | **PPV rises, NPV falls**; sensitivity and specificity do not change
! Cut-off lowered so more people test positive | Sensitivity up, specificity down
# 2 by 2 measures | biostats
! **Cohort** study measure | **Relative risk**: incidence in exposed ÷ incidence in unexposed
! **Case-control** study measure | **Odds ratio**: ad ÷ bc
! **Number needed to treat** | 1 ÷ absolute risk reduction
# Study designs | biostats
! Start with **disease vs no disease**, look back at exposures | Case-control
! Follow **exposed vs unexposed** forward | Cohort
! **Snapshot** of prevalence at one time | Cross-sectional
! Same people get **both treatments** with a washout | Crossover trial
# Bias | biostats
! Screening finds disease earlier but **death comes at the same time** | Lead-time bias
! Screening picks up **slow-growing** tumours | Length-time bias
! People **remember exposures differently** after disease | Recall bias
! Subjects **change behaviour because they are watched** | Hawthorne effect
# Statistical tests and errors | biostats
! Compare **two means** | t-test
! Compare **three or more means** | ANOVA
! Compare **proportions** or categories | Chi-square
! **Type I error** | Saying there is a difference when there is none: alpha
! **Type II error** | Missing a real difference: beta; power = 1 minus beta
! Confidence interval for a **ratio includes 1** | Not significant
# Incidence and prevalence | biostats
! **New cases** over a time period | Incidence
! New treatment **prolongs survival without cure** | Prevalence rises
# Prevention | biostats
! **Prevent disease** happening | Primary prevention: vaccines
! **Screen** for early disease | Secondary prevention
! **Reduce harm** from established disease | Tertiary prevention
# Consent and capacity | ethics
! Adult with capacity **refuses life-saving treatment** | Respect the refusal
! Patient lacks capacity with **no advance directive** | Surrogate decides on **substituted judgement**
! **Emergency**, no time and no surrogate | Treat under implied consent
# Confidentiality | ethics
! Patient threatens a **specific person** | Duty to warn and protect: Tarasoff
! **Reportable infection** | Report to public health even without consent
# Minors | ethics
! Teenager asks about **contraception or STI treatment** | Can consent without parents in most US states
! Parents of a child refuse a **blood transfusion in an emergency** | Treat the child
# Communication | ethics, behav
! Patient asks **"Am I going to die?"** | Explore their concerns with an open question before answering
! **Angry** patient | Acknowledge the emotion first
! Patient speaks **another language** | Use a professional interpreter, not family
# Medical errors and safety | ethics
! An error **reached the patient** | Disclose it honestly and apologise
! Multiple small failures line up | Swiss cheese model: **root cause analysis**
! Abuse of a child or elder suspected | **Report** to protective services
